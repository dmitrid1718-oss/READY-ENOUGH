Industrial resilienceApril 2026

A presidential determination identified long lead times, limited domestic capacity and import dependence for transformers and other grid equipment as national-defense vulnerabilities.

Classification: documented policy action; not evidence of an imminent attack.

🇺🇸 United States · Transformers for Ukraine

International assistance2022 onward

The US provided electrical components to support Ukraine's damaged grid. The Department of Energy told Congress in 2023 that no equipment critical to operation of the US grid was shipped. The remaining domestic spare inventory is not established by these sources.

Classification: documented transfers; total available US reserves remain unverified.

Evidence rules

Documented: government-origin source, publication date and identified action.

Routine / change: do not classify a brochure, routine drill or budget line as unusual without a historical baseline and corroboration.

Unknown: insufficient data remains clearly labeled. Missing information is not evidence of concealment.

Attribution: never infer who caused or will cause an attack from preparedness activity.

Scope: manually curated reference edition. No automatic monitoring or live threat scoring is claimed.
