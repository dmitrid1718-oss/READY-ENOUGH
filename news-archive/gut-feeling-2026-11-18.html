Just a Gut Feeling — November 18

Confirmed / reported — US aviation and digital systems: On November 18, AP reported the FAA paused Juniper router installations and disabled recently installed units after investigators connected a router deployment in Arkansas with last week's ground stop affecting 17 air-traffic control centers. The agency is reverting to earlier equipment while testing proceeds. The NSA described its November 17 internal network degradation as routine maintenance; officials said essential capabilities continued. US officials said there is no evidence the recent outages were coordinated cyberattacks. That is important counterevidence to a single-attack narrative.

Reported — international software risk: Reuters, November 18 reports US and South Korean authorities urging rapid installation of Oracle Java security fixes released November 17. This matters to organizations operating affected software, not as a reason for households to assume their electricity or water will fail.

Interpretation and proportionate action: Keep an offline copy of important phone numbers and confirm flights with the airline before travel. Administrators should check vendor advisories and patch affected Java deployments. No special household stockpiling is indicated.
